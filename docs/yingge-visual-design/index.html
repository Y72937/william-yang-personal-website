Scene, shader & rhythm

I collected building facade references, brought them into one visual style, and made a rhythm-wave UI that responds to the music.

A consistent street

I used AI assistance to create a shader that standardizes pixel size, reduces color differences, and refines edges. Rooftop colors extend into irregular vertical streaks that fade upward.

Making the beat visible

I built the glowing wave with layered gradients and Unity’s VertexHelper. Its amplitude, width, and brightness change with the beat.
