02 / 03 · Difficulty & pacing

Testing the First Six Levels

I tested whether players could get through the opening levels with normal controls, sensible strategy, and basic guidance, while still finding a challenge.

Finding where players got stuck

I reviewed enemy setups, wave timing, resources, starting warships, and technology unlocks. Recordings, logs, surveys, and conversations helped me locate the difficult points.

Looking for the cause

I looked at whether the problem came from enemy strength, short gaps between waves, limited resources, unclear guidance, or ship growth. I then proposed changes and checked them through further tests.

Difficulty testing and optimization work carried out as part of the Coco Laugh Studio team.
