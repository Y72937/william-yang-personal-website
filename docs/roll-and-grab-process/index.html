Visual style & sketches

I chose gray and gold, familiar symbols, and clear information positions. I wanted players to think about their choices rather than struggle to read the cards.

What I took away

Playtesting helped me improve both the rules and the way the cards communicate. The design changed when I watched how people actually played.

Dice imagery and symbols include sourced assets; Figma, Pinterest, and Noun Project are credited in the final portfolio.
