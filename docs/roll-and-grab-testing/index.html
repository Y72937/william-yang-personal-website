04 / 05 · Player testing

Player testing

Watching players hesitate, miss opportunities, or find shortcuts showed me what my rules actually encouraged.

More people involved

I changed the timed, turn-based version into simultaneous play for all three players.

More ways to succeed

I added Permanent Cards and non-number Objectives so success did not depend only on speed with numbers.

A fair start

Players shuffle their own Hand Cards each round and pick them up only after the dice holder opens. This prevents an advantage from arranging cards early.
