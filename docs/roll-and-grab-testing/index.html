Around the Table

Watching people play showed me things I had missed when writing the rules. I paid attention to waiting, hesitation, confusing cards, and the ways players tried to gain an advantage.

What testing changed

I changed the timed two-player version into three-player simultaneous play so everyone could stay involved. Later tests led me to add more scoring options, clarify the card information, and keep hands face down until the dice were revealed.
