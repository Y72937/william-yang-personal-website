Card design & readability

I kept the condition, required Hand Card, scoring limit, and reward in consistent positions so players could find them quickly.

A choice, not just a race

One Hand Card can match two Objectives. Players choose between a safer reward and a bigger risk. Familiar ranks, suits, and player colors make each move easier to recognize.
