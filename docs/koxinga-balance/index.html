Balancing Ships & Choices

I worked on eight warship types and broke down 16 attributes, including armor, damage, range, and fire rate. I used Python models and combat calculations to compare ship roles, growth curves, and technology paths.

Giving each ship a role

I compared strengths, weaknesses, and counters so that different ship combinations could offer useful choices. I also considered upgrade costs, prerequisites, and the timing of unlocks.

✳

What I learned

A small numerical change can affect when a level becomes possible or whether an upgrade feels worth taking. I learned to check the calculation against the player’s experience.

My contribution focused on game planning, testing, and numerical balance within the team.
