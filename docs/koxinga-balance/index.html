Warships & technology

I clarified the roles of eight warship types and tuned 16 combat attributes, including health, armor, damage, range, and firing interval.

Upgrades with a trade-off

Technology bonuses reinforce ship roles and tactical choices. For example, additional cannons also increase ammunition consumption, while armor-piercing options counter heavily armored ships.
