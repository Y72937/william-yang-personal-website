Cultural inspiration

I wanted the game to carry the energy of dancing together, from the rhythm to the way the group moves through a street.

From movement to animation

I studied the two batons, forward leaps, swings, and exaggerated limb movements. I commissioned keyframe animations from these references, then applied them to character models in Unity.

Movement reference and animation integration by me; keyframe animation commissioned from an animation team.
