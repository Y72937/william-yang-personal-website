What Is Yingge?

Yingge is a group dance from southern China. I was drawn to the strong movements, shared rhythm, and energy of a street procession.

↔

The strength of moving together

In my visual research, I placed Yingge beside the Māori haka to compare the intensity of a group performance. The comparison helped me explain the feeling I wanted to explore, while keeping Yingge's own movements and cultural setting at the center.
