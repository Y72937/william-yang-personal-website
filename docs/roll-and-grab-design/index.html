Table top Game-Roll And Grab

I designed Roll And Grab for three players to react to the same dice and choose between safer rewards and riskier opportunities. A game takes no more than 20 minutes.

Fast decisions around a table

Players match Hand Cards to Objective Cards to earn tokens. After five rounds, the player with the most tokens wins.
