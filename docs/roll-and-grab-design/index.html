Roll And Grab

A basketball rebound gave me the idea: everyone sees the same signal, but each person has to judge when to move. I turned that moment into a simultaneous tabletop game for three players.

Reading the cards

Each Objective Card brings together a condition, the required Hand Card, scoring places, and a token reward. I kept these details in consistent positions so players could find them during a fast round.

One card, more than one choice

A Hand Card can fit more than one Objective. Players have to decide where to use it, balancing the reward against the competition.

I designed the game, rules, and card layouts, and organized playtests. Some visual assets were sourced from Pinterest and Noun Project.
