YINGGE: STREETBEAT

The part of Yingge that stayed with me was the feeling of a whole group moving to the same beat. I wanted to find a way for a player to take part in that feeling.

Listening before designing

I visited villages and heritage sites, spoke with performers and researchers, and joined rehearsals and performances. I recorded movement, costume details, and drumming rhythms through notes and interviews.

✳

Beyond the game

My interviews became articles and a report on young people and Yingge. I also wrote a paper about intangible heritage in China’s game industry.
