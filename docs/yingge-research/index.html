Video Game-YINGGE STREETBEAT

I made a rhythm-action game inspired by Chaoshan Yingge dance. Players switch between pedestrians, follow the beat, and bring a growing group into one synchronized dance.

Introduction Video + Trailer↗Available at Itch.io↗

Starting with the dance

I looked at Yingge’s shared rhythm, synchronized movements, and street processions. Those collective qualities became the starting point for the game.

Solo project · Game design, programming, level design & UI design.
