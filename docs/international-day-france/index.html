02 / 02 · Leading the team

International Day for France

The next year, I led our France team. We sold baguettes, fried snacks, macarons, scented sachets, and perfume, and placed second in the school.

Letting people try it first

I was also one of our main sellers. Visitors could try a scent first; if they liked it, I introduced our other fragrances. Our perfume brought in over RMB 3,000 for the booth.

Everyone joined in

My favorite moment was seeing my teammates and our teacher mixing perfume together, then helping each other introduce it to visitors.

Team leader · School-wide second place
