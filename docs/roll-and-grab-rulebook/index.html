Rules & game flow

Writing the rulebook helped me clarify each round, how the cards relate to each other, and how players earn tokens.

Five rounds, shared dice

Shuffle the Hand Cards, reveal three Objectives, then lift the dice holder together. Players commit cards to Objectives; correct and fast responses earn tokens. A tie continues until there is a winner.
