Clash of Armadas: Koxinga

I have loved history since childhood, especially the difficult decisions behind a historical figure’s story. Zheng Chenggong made me wonder what it would be like to take part in that moment rather than only read about it.

History, experienced
through play.

A historical question

After discussing the idea with game developer Simon Yu, I wanted to explore how a game could let players experience the pressures and choices of the period.

My role

As a Game Planner Intern at Coco Laugh Studio, I worked on difficulty testing and numerical balance for this team project.
