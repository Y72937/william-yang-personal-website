From Dance to Game

I built a rhythm game in Unity where players switch between pedestrians, hit the beat to expand their active range, and draw nearby passersby into the dance.

Hit the beat. Build the group.

I made the growing group the center of the interaction. The player moves through the street by changing which pedestrian they control, while rhythm input affects how many people can join.

My part

I worked on the game design, levels, interface, and Unity implementation. I researched the dance poses and applied commissioned keyframe animations to the characters.

Made with Unity and C#. Programming was assisted by AI tools.

Character and environment models include third-party asset packs. Dance keyframe animations were commissioned and integrated by me.
