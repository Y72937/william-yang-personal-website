Core loop & group control

The dance group is the playable character. Shared rhythm becomes a way to move together and bring more people into the performance.

Switch. Hit the beat. Build the group.

Players switch between pedestrians and hit the beat to expand their active range. Pedestrians inside it join the dance. Activating all three temples completes the level.
