01 / 02 · Where it started

Bendy go!

I wore a large scoliosis brace as a teenager. I remember the restricted movement, but also how awkward I felt about wearing it around other people.

Listening to other patients

Volunteering at the Guangdong Xin Miao Scoliosis Prevention Center gave me more chances to listen to patients and help them communicate with staff. I wanted to make looking after the body feel less discouraging.

A reason to make something

Those experiences led me to explore a wearable prototype and a movement-based game. I wanted to give students a more inviting way to engage with movement and spinal health.

This is the brace I wore during treatment, separate from my Bendy go! prototype.
