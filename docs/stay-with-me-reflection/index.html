Game jam reflections

This was my first game jam. Getting the interaction areas right took longer than I expected: coordinates and the distance needed to investigate an object mattered in every room.

Our design intention

We started with collecting words and combining them to solve puzzles. We built the story around communicating with a frightened child, asking players to look, listen, and piece together an explanation.
